## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an executive, highly reliable aesthetic engineered for enterprise fleet operations, dispatch logistics, and luxury tour management. It marries the refined grandeur of bespoke travel—reminiscent of Maharaja-class vehicle interiors and premier long-distance cruisers—with the exacting precision of modern logistics SaaS.

The target audience encompasses dispatch controllers, fleet directors, regional travel managers, and VIP concierge desk leads who demand zero-ambiguity oversight, instantaneous status awareness, and robust control over high-value mobility assets. The visual tone must evoke institutional trust, prompt operational authority, and meticulous craft.

To achieve this, the system leverages a **Corporate / Modern** framework heightened by low-contrast structural grids, surgical micro-interactions, crisp slate delineation, and warm amber gold highlights. It bypasses casual consumer decoration in favor of high-density clarity, executive data hierarchy, and ergonomic multi-pane workspaces.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around authoritative high-contrast dark foundational tones juxtaposed against luminous utility canvases and refined amber-gold accents.

- **Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep Obsidian anchors navigation sidebars, high-priority headings, primary action buttons, and dominant operational matrices. It projects executive weight and discipline.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`)**: Warm Amber Gold represents the luxury Maharaja tier, highlighted fleet assets, active telematics markers, and key call-to-action checkpoints without triggering panic or distraction.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Emerald Green serves as an operational trust badge for verified fleet diagnostics, active driver compliance, GPS telemetry lock, and certified honest KM billing verification.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Slate neutral governs supporting metric descriptions, meta labels, borders, and contextual column identifiers.

In light mode, surfaces rely on ultra-clean slate canvas backdrops (`#F8FAFC`) with crisp, non-distracting hairline borders (`#E2E8F0`) to segregate dense logistics data tables, multi-tier schedules, and side-by-side inspect drawers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headers, brand anchors, table columns, and status identifiers with **Inter** for dense transactional bodies, telematics readouts, and long-form passenger manifest details.

- **Headers & Display**: Plus Jakarta Sans gives a contemporary, authoritative posture. Tight letter-spacing keeps titles compact and readable on dense enterprise screens.
- **Data & Body**: Inter guarantees optimal tabular numbers, pristine alignment across dense grid systems, and unmatched readability at 13px and 14px in high-speed dispatch scenarios.
- **Numbers & Metrics**: Quantitative data points (such as Odometer readings, Billing rate per KM, GPS coordinates, and vehicle counts) leverage `metric-val` using tabular numerals for immediate vertical scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around a flexible, high-efficiency grid optimized for operational visibility and split-screen task management:

- **Desktop (1200px and up)**: A fixed 260px administrative left rail, paired with a 12-column dynamic work canvas using `1.5rem` gutters. When inspecting an asset, a 420px contextual right preview pane slides into view, docking telemetry cards, KM rate breakdowns, and real-time live route status.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: The left sidebar collapses into an iconified 72px rail. Multi-column tables shift to prioritized scroll configurations, and detail drawers overlay the right side of the screen rather than split-docking.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: A unified single-column layout using `margin-mobile` (1rem). Dense tabular data gracefully shifts into self-contained operational cards with swipe-to-reveal quick actions (e.g., Call Driver, Flag Discrepancy).
- **Rhythm**: Spacing follows an intentional 4px/8px rhythm. Compact margins inside table cells (`space-sm` vertical, `space-md` horizontal) ensure maximum operational data visibility per screen fold without cognitive clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is established using **low-contrast outlines** paired with subtle, micro-calibrated ambient shadows. This keeps the enterprise interface crisp and prevents visual fatigue during extended operational monitoring:

- **Base Layer (Level 0)**: Background canvas (`#F8FAFC`), entirely flat.
- **Surface Layer (Level 1)**: Operational cards, table containers, and filter toolbars use `#FFFFFF` encapsulated by a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` and an ultra-subtle ambient shadow (`box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`).
- **Interactive Hover & Flyout (Level 2)**: Action menus, floating tooltips, and elevated card hovers lift with an amber-tinted or slate-tinted ambient spread (`box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`).
- **Overlay & Modal Drawers (Level 3)**: Live fleet inspection drawers, VIP manifest editors, and billing reconciliation modals feature high-depth delineation (`box-shadow: -8px 0 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft (`1`)** shape language. 
- Standard buttons, form controls, chips, and small cards employ `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px) corner rounding.
- Modal windows, large container frames, and preview panes adhere to `0.5rem` (8px).
- Status badges and micro verification tags are softly chamfered at `4px` rather than fully pill-shaped, retaining an industrial, executive character suited for fleet hardware, travel permits, and logistics telemetry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep Obsidian (`#0F172A`) solid background, crisp white typography, 1px border matching `#0F172A`. Transitions on hover to `#1E293B`.
- **Secondary / Luxury Action**: Rich Amber Gold (`#D97706`) fill with white text, or Gold ghost variant (`#FEF3C7` background with `#B45309` text) for VIP Maharaja booking workflows.
- **Outline / Utility**: `#FFFFFF` background, `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text. Hover adds a slight slate tint (`#F8FAFC`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Fleet Verification & Honest KM**: Emerald Green (`#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` text, `#A7F3D0` border). Accompanied by a leading micro checkmark icon.
- **Vehicle Tier**: Deep slate background (`#0F172A`) with amber gold accents for Maharaja Force Tempo Travellers, Urbania executive cruisers, and luxury vans.
- **Dispatch Status**: Amber warning chips (`#FEF3C7` / `#B45309`) for 'In-Transit / Unverified KM' and red alerts (`#FEF2F2` / `#DC2626`) for 'Overdue / Inspection Required'.

### Data Tables
- Header cells styled with `label-sm` in all caps, `#64748B` typography, with a bottom hairline border (`#E2E8F0`).
- Rows feature alternating or clean hover highlights (`#F8FAFC`).
- Numerical columns (KM run, rate per KM, GPS speed) align right using tabular figures.
- Direct row-level action triggers for "Inspect Asset", "Print Trip Sheet", and "Audit Fuel/KM Log".

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form inputs feature a clean 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), with a focus state switching to `#0F172A` outline accompanied by an amber glow ring (`0 0 0 2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)`).
- Checkboxes and radios adopt sharp corporate styling with obsidian active fills and clean white ticks.

### Cards & Dispatch Modules
- Clean metric stat cards displaying: Total Active Fleet, Maharaja Vehicles Dispatched, Honest KM Metric Variance (<0.1%), and Current Revenue. 
- Upper border accents using 2px Amber Gold highlights on luxury asset cards.
- Integrated mini route-telemetry cards displaying live origin, mid-way checkpoints, and terminal ETA.
